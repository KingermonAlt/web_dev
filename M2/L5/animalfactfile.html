Snow Leopard

Type: Big Cat

Lives in: Central Asian mountains

Diet: Deer, goats, and wild sheep

Cool fact: Leaps up to 15 metres!

Home: Rocky, snowy mountain ranges.

Survival: Thick fur and a long tail keep it warm and balanced.

Fun fact: Its wide paws act like snowshoes.

Golden Eagle

Type: Bird of prey

Lives in: Cliffs and open countryside

Diet: Rabbits and small mammals

Cool fact: Can spot prey from far away.

Home: High cliffs and remote mountain ledges.

Survival: Powerful talons and broad wings help it hunt.

Fun fact: It can dive at over 240 km/h.

Green Turtle

Type: Sea turtle

Lives in: Warm tropical oceans

Diet: Seagrass and algae

Cool fact: Returns to the beach where it hatched.

Home: Coral reefs, shallow bays, and sandy beaches.

Survival: Its shell protects it while strong flippers carry it across oceans.

Fun fact: It can hold its breath for several hours while resting.

Arctic Fox

Type: Small mammal

Lives in: Arctic tundra

Diet: Lemmings, birds, and berries

Cool fact: Its coat changes colour with the seasons.

Home: Snowy dens beneath the frozen ground.

Survival: Thick fur and furry paws help it stay warm.

Fun fact: It can hear prey moving under the snow.

Giant Pacific Octopus

Type: Marine invertebrate

Lives in: Rocky Pacific seafloors

Diet: Crabs, fish, and shellfish

Cool fact: It has three hearts and blue blood.

Home: Caves and crevices in cold coastal waters.

Survival: It changes colour and texture to hide from danger.

Fun fact: Each arm can taste and explore independently.

Ruby-throated Hummingbird

Type: Tiny bird

Lives in: Gardens and woodland edges

Diet: Nectar and tiny insects

Cool fact: Its wings beat dozens of times each second.

Home: Forest edges across North America.

Survival: Fast flight lets it hover and escape predators.

Fun fact: It can fly forwards, backwards, and sideways.
