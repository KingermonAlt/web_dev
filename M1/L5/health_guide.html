💪 Why Your Body Needs Exercise

Your body is like a supercar - the more you use it, the stronger it gets!

Exercise helps your heart, muscles, and brain stay strong. Try to move your body every day by walking, dancing, stretching, or playing a favorite sport.

☀️ Quick Health Tips

  • 🍎 Eat fruits and vegetables
  • 💧 Drink plenty of water
  • 😴 Get enough sleep each night
  • 🧼 Wash your hands often
  • 🪥 Brush your teeth twice a day
